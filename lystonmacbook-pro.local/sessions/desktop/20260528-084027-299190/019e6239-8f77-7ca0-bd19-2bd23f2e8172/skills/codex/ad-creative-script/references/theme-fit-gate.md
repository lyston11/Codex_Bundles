# Theme Fit Gate

Use this before creative imagination, especially when the user says the idea does not fit the theme, the script is off-topic, or the imagination feels like random weirdness.

## Core Rule

Creativity is only valid after theme fit. A strange device, world rule, genre, or metaphor must be authorized by the communication job and the product/category's real role in people's lives.

If the creative route cannot answer "why is this a good ad for this exact category/product?", reject it before scripting.

Do not reduce every ad to functional selling. Brand films, launch films, trust films, and performance ads have different jobs.

## Communication Job First

Classify the job before evaluating theme fit:

| Job | Primary role | Creative can emphasize | Must still keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand building | Long-term memory, trust, worldview, emotional salience | Human truth, brand belief, distinctive assets, category entry points | Clear category/brand fit and future buying memory |
| Product marketing | Preference for a specific product | Product truth, routine, usage, reason to believe | Product role and credible claims |
| Trust / proof | Reduce doubt and risk | Quality system, sourcing, certification, traceability, expert context | Compliance and specificity |
| Launch | Make a new product/position memorable | New ritual, new visual law, new product role | Product introduction and category clarity |
| Performance / conversion | Action now | Hook, problem, proof, CTA | Clear offer and claim safety |
| Hybrid | Balance brand and product | Brand world plus product reason | Do not let one erase the other |
 
For brand building, the product does not need to be a literal cause in every beat. The brand/category must be emotionally and culturally necessary: the story should build a memory that later helps the audience recognize, trust, or prefer the brand in a relevant category entry point.

## Theme-Fit Check

Before choosing any route, fill:

```text
Product/category:
User-given topic:
Communication job:
Brand role:
Audience decision logic:
Category entry point:
Real purchase/use or memory moment:
Category trust barrier:
Approved or plausible product truth:
Brand truth / worldview:
What the creative device dramatizes:
Why the brand/product/category is necessary:
What would be lost if the brand/product/category were removed:
What must not be claimed:
```

Do not proceed if the route is mainly:

- A visual metaphor with weak product causality.
- A fantasy that could fit any category.
- A location swap with the same generic message.
- A parenting emotion with no milk-powder-specific brand, category, or buying-memory reason.
- A dramatic world that says only "care" but not brand belief, trust, feeding routine, nutrition support, sourcing, formulation, quality, parental confidence, or category entry memory.

## Milk Powder Theme Fit

For milk powder, first identify the job.

For a **brand film**, imagination may connect to:

- **Brand belief**: what this brand believes about growth, care, parents, childhood, patience, science, nature, trust, or everyday courage.
- **Long-term trust**: not a proof dump, but a durable feeling that the brand is careful, responsible, warm, or expert.
- **Parent-child worldview**: how the brand sees modern parenting without exploiting anxiety.
- **Category entry memory**: shelf choice, family advice, daily feeding, first transition, travel, daycare, illness recovery, picky eating, returning to work.
- **Distinctive brand assets**: repeatable line, ritual, sound, object, visual law, character, place, or format.
- **Cultural tension**: parenting under judgment, over-information, comparison, expert overload, perfection pressure.

For a **product marketing / trust proof film**, imagination should connect to at least one of these:

- **Trust**: why parents can rely on the product, within approved proof.
- **Feeding routine**: the repeatable action of preparing, measuring, mixing, feeding, and choosing.
- **Nutrition support**: approved nutrition/formula/sourcing facts without overclaiming.
- **Digestive comfort / absorption / growth support**: only if the brand has substantiated claims; otherwise use general support language.
- **Parental confidence**: reducing decision noise without insulting other parents or exploiting fear.
- **Safety / quality system**: traceability, manufacturing rigor, packaging seal, batch code, cold chain, certification, expert guidance when allowed.
- **Child's everyday life**: product supports normal daily rhythm, not miraculous transformation.

Do not build milk powder ads mainly around:

- A random quest, desert journey, magic object, cosmic mission, or visual spectacle where milk powder is just a prop.
- "Finding a green place", "growing a tree", "lighting a path", "unlocking a future", "building a world" unless the brand belief, category memory, or product/trust role is concrete and necessary.
- Perfect growth, guaranteed intelligence, immunity, height, or disease prevention.
- Fear that parents are failing without the product.

## Topic Binding

If the user gives a topic such as "milk powder + desert + oasis", bind the topic to the communication job before ideation.

Good binding examples:

- Brand film: Desert = modern parenting pressure, comparison, or the dry noise around "perfect growth"; Oasis = the brand's belief in steady care, child-specific rhythm, or a calmer parenting worldview.
- Product/trust film: Desert = parental decision scarcity or feeding uncertainty; Oasis = a reliable feeding routine, verified quality system, or child-specific daily rhythm.
- Sand = noisy advice, unverified claims, or unstable routines.
- Water = preparation / mixing / daily nourishment only when tied to the actual product ritual.
- Map / compass = choosing and repeating the right feeding standard, or the brand's point of view on how parents find confidence.

Bad binding examples:

- Milk powder creates a literal oasis with no product proof.
- Parents wander through desert because it looks cinematic.
- Child's personal oasis grows as a vague metaphor for growth.
- Remote green imagery replaces the actual feeding occasion.

## Route Kill Questions

Ask before scripting:

1. What is the communication job: brand, product, trust/proof, launch, performance, or hybrid?
2. Is the brand/product/category role visible before the final packshot?
3. For brand film, does the world rule build a relevant brand belief, distinctive asset, or category entry memory?
4. For product/proof film, does the world rule change the feeding/choosing/trust moment, not just scenery?
5. Could this same idea sell diapers, education, insurance, or bottled water? If yes, rebuild.
6. Can the viewer state either the brand belief or the milk-powder reason after one viewing?
7. Are claims compliant and not miraculous?
8. Is the most memorable image also brand/category/product-relevant?
9. Does every major beat return to the selected job rather than free-floating spectacle?

## Repair Moves

If the idea is imaginative but off-theme:

- Move product action earlier.
- Turn the creative device into a product-relevant test, standard, routine, or proof.
- If the job is brand building, turn the device into a brand belief, category entry memory, or distinctive brand asset instead of forcing a functional demo.
- For product/proof films, replace vague growth metaphor with feeding-specific behavior.
- For product/proof films, add product causality: measure, mix, seal, batch, routine, verified source, repeatable step.
- Remove any image that could belong to bottled water, education, travel, insurance, or generic parenting unless the brand/category binding is unmistakable.
- Make the end frame resolve the selected job: brand memory, product reason, trust proof, launch meaning, or CTA.
