# Script Formats

Use this before full scripting:

```text

Product Role:
Why It Sells:

```

## Brand Film / TVC

Default format:

| Time | World-rule event | Visual | VO / Dialogue | Sound | Product role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00-05s | The impossible rule appears immediately. | Hook image or tension | Minimal | Sonic hook | Product absent or teased |
| 05-15s | The rule changes how a normal situation works. | Escalation, not just setup | Set up insight | Rhythm begins | Product enters as cause |
| 15-25s | Product participates in the rule. | Demonstration / transformation | Benefit bridge | Music lift | Product visibly matters |
| 25-30s | The rule resolves into brand memory. | End frame | Tagline | Sonic logo | Product hero |

For 60s, add:
- 00-10s: human truth already distorted by the world rule
- 10-25s: impossible device/world escalates
- 25-45s: product-enabled transformation, not ordinary montage
- 45-55s: emotional landing through the same world rule
- 55-60s: brand lockup that preserves the recurring asset

For high-imagination work, add a pre-script beat density table:

```text
World rule:
Recurring asset:
Beat 1 transformation:
Beat 2 transformation:
Beat 3 transformation:
Beat 4 transformation:
Beat 5 transformation:
Percent of screen time governed by world rule:
```

Do not write a full script until each beat has a transformation beyond ordinary daily life.

## Short Video Performance Ad

Use for Douyin/TikTok/Reels/Shorts or paid social:

```text
Hook options:
1. Pattern interrupt
2. Confession / unpopular truth
3. Before-after contradiction
4. Visual impossibility
5. Social proof / challenge

Script:
00-03s Hook
03-08s Problem in a concrete scene
08-15s Product demo or proof
15-22s Transformation / payoff
22-30s CTA or social behavior

Variants:
- Hard sell
- Soft story
- UGC-style
- Surreal visual
- Expert / proof-led
```

## Dialogue And VO

Prefer less VO. Let the visual idea carry the film.

Use VO when:
- The insight needs a poetic frame.
- The product proof needs clarity.
- The brand has a distinct voice.

Avoid:
- Explaining every visual.
- Generic lines such as "for every moment" or "more than just".
- Empty superlatives without proof.

## Product Claims

Use only claims supplied by the user as factual. If claims are inferred, mark them as claim placeholders.

For sensitive categories:
- Baby / infant: avoid fear, superiority claims, medical implication, or guaranteed development outcomes.
- Food / nutrition: avoid disease prevention or treatment claims unless approved.
- Finance: avoid guaranteed returns.
- Health / beauty: avoid permanent or clinical claims unless approved.
