# Industry Adapters

Use this to make the creative method fit the category. A good ad for a milk powder brand, a SaaS tool, and a luxury watch should not share the same emotional engine.

## Category Adapter Template

For any industry, fill:

```text
Decision logic:
Audience hidden pressure:
Trust barrier:
Proof style:
Category cliches:
Promising creative territories:
Risk / compliance:
Best formats:
```

## Baby / Parenting / Milk Powder

- **Decision logic**: trust, safety, development support, parental confidence, social proof.
- **Hidden pressure**: parents are not only choosing a product; they are managing guilt, doubt, family judgment, and fear of making the wrong small decision.
- **Trust barrier**: exaggerated claims, fearmongering, fake science, perfect-family fantasy.
- **Proof style**: approved nutrition facts, sourcing, manufacturing rigor, pediatric/nutrition expert context when allowed, real routines.
- **Brand-building role**: build long-term trust, parental confidence, a distinctive view of childhood/growth/care, and future shelf-choice memory. A milk-powder brand film does not need to behave like a product demo, but it must still feel unmistakably about infant/child nutrition, parenting confidence, or a brand philosophy of care.
- **Avoid**: guaranteed intelligence/height/immunity claims, panic narratives, sick-child implications, "other parents are irresponsible" framing.
- **Creative territories**: visible care architecture, future self as gentle witness, family micro-ritual, doubt becoming quiet, everyday invisible labor made visible.
- **Brand territories**: a brand that respects each child's rhythm, a parent learning to trust steady care over comparison, growth as a relationship rather than a race, science and tenderness as companions, daily feeding as a small repeatable promise, family advice noise becoming quieter, a distinctive ritual that can recur across campaigns.
- **High-imagination territories**: parental doubt as an institution, nutrition trust as a physical law, a family routine treated as a mission-control event, a spoon that changes gravity/time/weather only when care is consistent, the product as passport between "today's care" and "tomorrow's ordinary courage".
- **High-imagination warning**: do not confuse tenderness with imagination. Night feeding, future letters, glowing growth, and soft family montage are low-ambition unless turned into an unexpected world rule.
- **Best formats**: 60s brand film, 30s TVC, mother/baby routine short video, trust-building explainer with cinematic metaphor.

## Food / Beverage

- **Decision logic**: sensory desire, ritual, trust, freshness, identity, shared occasion.
- **Hidden pressure**: people want food to say something about care, taste, status, belonging, or self-control.
- **Trust barrier**: generic naturalness, overproduced appetite shots, vague health halo.
- **Proof style**: ingredient origin, process, sensory details, occasion truth, texture/sound.
- **Creative territories**: taste as weather/event, a table ritual, ingredient journey with a twist, small craving treated as epic.
- **Best formats**: 15-30s sensory film, social ritual, recipe/demo, seasonal campaign.

## Beauty / Personal Care

- **Decision logic**: transformation, identity, confidence, efficacy, sensory feel.
- **Hidden pressure**: people do not just want to look better; they want control over how the world reads them.
- **Trust barrier**: unrealistic beauty, fake before/after, pseudo-science, generic empowerment.
- **Proof style**: visible texture, user routine, ingredient role, approved claim, time-bound result when substantiated.
- **Creative territories**: mirror as unreliable narrator, skin/hair as landscape, confidence as behavior not slogan, ritual before entering public life.
- **Best formats**: 15-30s demo, creator-led routine, premium brand film, before/after only with honest framing.

## Health / Wellness / Medical Adjacent

- **Decision logic**: risk reduction, authority, empathy, ease, credibility.
- **Hidden pressure**: people often want dignity and normalcy as much as treatment or improvement.
- **Trust barrier**: overclaiming, fear exploitation, miracle cure tone.
- **Proof style**: clinically approved claims, expert context, mechanism explained simply, lived experience.
- **Creative territories**: invisible burden made visible, everyday life reclaimed, care without drama, small independence restored.
- **Best formats**: trust film, patient story, explainer, pharmacist/doctor-facing material if appropriate.

## Finance / Insurance

- **Decision logic**: trust, risk, future security, control, competence.
- **Hidden pressure**: money decisions expose fear of being naive, irresponsible, or unprepared.
- **Trust barrier**: vague prosperity, guaranteed return implication, umbrella metaphors, happy future montage.
- **Proof style**: transparent mechanism, scenario planning, regulatory-safe language, human decision support.
- **Creative territories**: future self conversation, risk as weather/map/gameboard, invisible safety net shown through behavior, anti-jargon honesty.
- **Best formats**: 30s trust TVC, explainer, testimonial-style stories, scenario social ads.

## Tech / Apps / Consumer Electronics

- **Decision logic**: usefulness, speed, identity, delight, ecosystem fit.
- **Hidden pressure**: people want technology to remove friction without making them feel less human.
- **Trust barrier**: feature list montage, floating UI, sci-fi data glow, productivity cliches.
- **Proof style**: real use case, time saved, task simplified, tactile product detail, integration.
- **Creative territories**: time behaving differently, app as backstage crew, devices as quiet companions, impossible interface made human.
- **Best formats**: product hero film, social demo, launch film, feature cutdowns.

## Home Appliances / Home Goods

- **Decision logic**: reliability, comfort, family rhythm, efficiency, durability.
- **Hidden pressure**: people want the home to run smoothly without becoming its unpaid manager.
- **Trust barrier**: sterile showroom, perfect home, exaggerated convenience, generic "smart life".
- **Proof style**: repeated daily moments, noise/cleanliness/time/energy details, durability cues.
- **Creative territories**: home as orchestra, chores disappearing from the story, objects gossiping about reliability, day rhythm restored.
- **Best formats**: 30s family-life TVC, product demo film, social before/after, retail cutdown.

## Auto / Mobility

- **Decision logic**: status, safety, freedom, performance, identity, family utility.
- **Hidden pressure**: mobility is about who people become when moving through the world.
- **Trust barrier**: empty roads, speed fantasy, generic mountain drive, anonymous luxury.
- **Proof style**: safety tech in human stakes, range/handling/space in real scenarios, design/material detail.
- **Creative territories**: roads as choices, car as family stage, city choreography, range anxiety transformed into freedom math.
- **Best formats**: 30-60s brand film, feature film, lifestyle cutdowns.

## Education / Learning

- **Decision logic**: future opportunity, confidence, measurable progress, trust in method.
- **Hidden pressure**: learners fear wasted effort and parents fear choosing the wrong path.
- **Trust barrier**: genius child montage, ladder/future key metaphors, exam panic.
- **Proof style**: learning method, progress evidence, teacher quality, student behavior change.
- **Creative territories**: confusion becoming navigable, future self sending clues, classroom as expedition, skill becoming visible.
- **Best formats**: 30s trust film, parent-facing explainer, student short video, testimonial.

## B2B / SaaS / Enterprise

- **Decision logic**: risk, ROI, integration, authority, internal buy-in.
- **Hidden pressure**: buyers need to look competent to peers and avoid becoming responsible for a bad system.
- **Trust barrier**: dashboard montage, buzzword soup, floating icons, "unlock productivity".
- **Proof style**: use case, before/after workflow, measurable result, integration detail, customer story.
- **Creative territories**: work chaos given physical form, hidden team heroism, decision room drama, system as control tower.
- **Best formats**: explainer film, sales enablement script, founder narrative, case-study ad.

## Luxury / Fashion / Premium Goods

- **Decision logic**: status, taste, craft, scarcity, self-image, cultural signal.
- **Hidden pressure**: people want distinction without looking like they are trying too hard.
- **Trust barrier**: marble emptiness, slow hands, hollow heritage, generic sophistication.
- **Proof style**: craft detail, provenance, material, design philosophy, cultural world.
- **Creative territories**: object with a private life, ritual of choosing, craft as obsession, time slowed for one detail.
- **Best formats**: 30-60s brand film, key visual campaign, launch film.

## Retail / E-commerce / Performance

- **Decision logic**: value, urgency, trust, convenience, social proof.
- **Hidden pressure**: shoppers want to feel clever, not manipulated.
- **Trust barrier**: fake scarcity, shouty discounts, chaotic benefit pile.
- **Proof style**: price, comparison, reviews, guarantee, visible demo.
- **Creative territories**: shopper as detective, deal as discovery, product trial ritual, "I wish I knew earlier" confession.
- **Best formats**: short video ad, UGC, offer-led cutdowns, live commerce script.

## Travel / Hospitality

- **Decision logic**: escape, identity, memory, ease, social bonding.
- **Hidden pressure**: people do not just want a place; they want proof their life can feel different there.
- **Trust barrier**: landmark montage, drone scenery, "discover yourself" cliche.
- **Proof style**: specific sensory moments, itinerary ease, local detail, guest experience.
- **Creative territories**: room as portal, local object as guide, future memory calling, itinerary as emotional arc.
- **Best formats**: brand film, destination short, social itinerary series.

## Games / Entertainment

- **Decision logic**: fun, identity, community, mastery, escape.
- **Hidden pressure**: audiences want a world they can belong to and retell.
- **Trust barrier**: generic action montage, hype words, ungrounded lore.
- **Proof style**: gameplay loop, character/world hook, community behavior, visual identity.
- **Creative territories**: player ritual, in-world ad, fourth-wall break, mundane life invaded by game logic.
- **Best formats**: trailer script, social launch stunt, character-led shorts.

## Public Service / Cause

- **Decision logic**: empathy, urgency, self-efficacy, social norm change.
- **Hidden pressure**: people resist feeling blamed or helpless.
- **Trust barrier**: guilt montage, shock without action, abstract statistics.
- **Proof style**: human story, clear action, local relevance, credible source.
- **Creative territories**: invisible consequence made visible, social norm flip, one small action as turning point.
- **Best formats**: 30-60s PSA, social challenge, OOH idea, explainer.
